Pricing FAQ

Can I combine a build package with a monthly care plan?

Yes — most clients start with a build package (4, 6 or 8 pages) and then add a Bronze, Silver or Gold monthly plan to keep the site maintained afterward.

How does extra-page pricing work?

The 8-page package covers up to 8 pages. Any page beyond that is charged at a flat Rs. 999 per additional page, added to your build package total.

Is hosting included?

Build packages cover design and development. Hosting support and ongoing maintenance are covered by our monthly Bronze, Silver and Gold care plans.

What payment methods do you accept?

Get in touch via our contact page and we'll share the available payment options for your plan or package.

Not sure which option is right for you?
